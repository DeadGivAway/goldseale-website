Leadership

Chuck Seale

President, The Gold Seale Companies, LLC

Builder. Developer. Steward Leader.

Chuck Seale has spent more than 20 years in real estate development and construction and has worked every side of it: owner's representative, preconstruction leader, and operator. He has overseen more than $1 billion in multifamily, mixed-use, LIHTC affordable housing, and commercial projects, including retail centers, restaurants, and grocery stores. He built one construction company from startup to more than $250 million in volume. At another, he managed a $700 million development pipeline across eight states and helped double contracts to over $300 million.

Today, Chuck leads The Gold Seale Companies, which includes GS Consulting, JS Builders, and its real estate investments. All of them run on the PEOPLE system (Proven Entrepreneurial Operating Principles for Leadership & Execution). The purpose is to provide affordable housing, with a mission to help those in greatest need first.
